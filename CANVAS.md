# Business Model Canvas – "Stammtisch" (working name)

Challenge: *Create a product or service that uses technology to foster genuine human connection
and/or counter the growing loneliness in our digital society.*
Organiser's note: "technology" = apps, platforms, AI, wearables. Examples they give:
**between generations, in the neighbourhood, within communities**. Our mixed-age matching
matches their example directly, so say so.

| Box | Content (from our whiteboard + gaps filled) |
|---|---|
| **Vision, mission & values** | **Vision:** less loneliness (Einsamkeit), everyone feels connected. **Mission:** create and strengthen weak ties in everyday moments, as easily as possible. **Values:** human-oriented, the platform is a means not an end, minimal screen time, safety & privacy first, diversity (mixing ages and backgrounds). |
| **Activities and services** | Maps add-on / web app: 1) opt in when choosing a restaurant or train, 2) AI matches on **shared interests** across **different age and background**, 3) same table/compartment + conversation starter, 4) 1-tap "meet again?", which leads to repeat meetings (weak tie to strong tie). Safety features: verification, public places only, blocking. |
| **Target groups** | **Users:** young people (16–30) who feel lonely: students, newcomers to a city, young professionals. Secondary: older people living alone (mixing generations). **Customers (who pay):** restaurants and cafés with empty seats off-peak; later SBB. |
| **Impact on people and society** | Less loneliness (a public-health issue). Weak ties build belonging. Mixing ages and backgrounds reduces polarisation. Environmental (secondary): uses existing spaces, fills off-peak seats (less daily-menu food waste), encourages local meeting on foot or by public transport. **Metric:** "would meet again" rate, repeat meetings, loneliness self-check. |
| **Competitors** | **Timeleft** (dinner with 6 strangers, runs in Zurich), Meetup, Bumble BFF, dating apps, communal tables, Plauderbänkli. **Our difference:** no planning, everyday moments you already have, **repetition with the same people**, deliberately mixed, lives in the maps app you already use. |
| **Revenue model & cost structure** | **Revenue:** free for users. Restaurants pay **per matched guest** (off-peak, pay only for results). Optional: promoted listings. Later: SBB, Gemeinden, health insurers (prevention). **Costs:** low. Software development & hosting, AI matching, verification service, marketing to reach critical mass in one city district. |
| **Resources** | Digital app / web app with Maps API integration, matching algorithm (AI), verification tool, a small team (dev, partnerships, community), restaurant network. |
| **Strategic partnerships** | Restaurants & cafés (seats, promotion), SBB (a "chat compartment" on trains), Google Maps / Swisstopo / search.ch (integration), universities & student associations (first users), Gemeinden & Pro Senectute (older users, legitimacy). |

**Next steps (for slide 3):** 3-month pilot with 5 restaurants in Zurich Kreis 4/5, recruit
first users via ETH/UZH student associations, measure the "would meet again" rate, then SBB talks.

## 2. What the jury probably wants
The 2025 win suggests the jury wants:
1. **A human problem, not only a carbon problem.** The winner combined an environmental
   issue (housing space per person, resource use) with a social one (loneliness, integration).
   Hit **both** halves of the brief.
2. **Swiss specificity.** Refer to Swiss realities: the Gemeinde, SBB, Migros/Coop, the
   high share of renters, multilingualism, direct democracy.
3. **A simple, concrete service** that a real user could try next month. Avoid large
   infrastructure.
4. **Light, credible use of tech.** The host is a consultancy, and the team called "GrüezAI" won,
   so it helps to have a clear digital enabler. AI should be a tool in the idea, not the idea itself.
5. **A memorable name and story.**

Without official criteria, assume the usual ones: **Impact · Innovation · Feasibility ·
Business model · Pitch quality**. Give each one explicit airtime in the pitch.

## 3. The 4-hour sprint plan
| Time | Step | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Decode the brief, assign roles (facilitator, researcher, business, designer, pitcher) | Brief on the wall, roles set |
| 0:15–0:45 | **Problem hunt**: each person writes 3 "How might we…" questions, then dot-vote | 1 problem + 1 target persona |
| 0:45–1:15 | **Ideate**: crazy-8s, then combine and pick one | 1 idea in one sentence |
| 1:15–1:45 | **Validate**: talk to 3 other participants/mentors as "users"; find 2–3 Swiss facts | Evidence + quotes |
| 1:45–2:45 | **Build**: value proposition, business model, impact estimate, mock-up (Figma/Canva/paper) | Clickable or drawn prototype |
| 2:45–3:30 | **Pitch deck** (max. 6–7 slides) | Deck |
| 3:30–4:00 | **Rehearse 3×** with a timer and anticipate Q&A | Tight pitch |

Rule: **freeze the idea at 1:45.** Most teams lose because they keep changing their idea.

## 4. Pitch structure (3–5 min)
1. **Hook** (15 s): a person and a moment. For example, "Meet Ruth, 78, alone in a 5-room flat in Winterthur…"
2. **Problem** (30 s): one Swiss number for scale and one human insight
3. **Solution** (60 s): what it is and how it works, shown with the mock-up
4. **Impact** (30 s): environmental **and** social, quantified even if roughly ("if 1% of…")
5. **Business model** (30 s): who pays (Gemeinde, housing co-op, insurer, retailer, users)
6. **Feasibility / next steps** (20 s): pilot partner, first 90 days
7. **Ask + close** (15 s): repeat the name and one-line vision

Prepare answers to these questions: *Who pays? Why hasn't this been done? How do you measure impact?
What's the rebound effect? What's your first pilot?*

## 5. Useful Swiss context (cite as approximate; check on the day if you can)
- **Net zero by 2050** is law (Climate and Innovation Act, accepted by referendum June 2023).
  The Electricity Supply Act on renewables was accepted in June 2024.
- **Transport** is the largest source of domestic greenhouse-gas emissions (about a third).
  **Buildings** (heating, many with oil/gas) are roughly a quarter.
- **A large share of the Swiss footprint is emitted abroad** (imported goods and food).
  This supports consumption-side ideas.
- **Food waste**: about 2.8 million tonnes a year along the food chain, a large part in
  households. Tischlein Deck Dich and Too Good To Go are existing players.
- Switzerland is a **nation of renters**. There is housing shortage and rising rents in cities,
  and floor space per person keeps increasing. This is the lever GrüezAI used.
- An **ageing society** with loneliness among older people, plus integration of newcomers.
  These fit the "social inclusion" angle.

## 6. Idea starters (sustainability × inclusion × Swiss)
- **Repair & share hub finder**: a Gemeinde-level map and booking tool for Repair Cafés, tool libraries
  and "Pumpipumpe" stickers, with volunteering credits for retirees and newcomers.
- **Grey-energy receipt**: a digital Migros/Coop receipt that shows the CO₂ of each purchase and
  suggests swaps, with a bonus in loyalty points.
- **Heat-pump neighbourhood co-op**: bundles building owners on a street to share
  planning and financing for replacing oil heating. Language support for foreign owners.
- **Last-mile surplus food by apprentices**: logistics-apprentice projects deliver
  retail surplus to food banks, which gives training value plus less waste.
- **"Room for a Coffee"**: a room-for-help swap between students and older people
  (proven model "Wohnen für Hilfe"). Improve it with matching and trust tools.
- **Commuter-carbon Gemeinde challenge**: towns compete on SBB/velo share. Rewards go to
  local associations (Vereine), which drives social cohesion.

Pick one and adapt it to the brief announced on the day. Don't force it.

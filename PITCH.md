# Pitch kit – "Maps add-on for real-life connection"

Official brief (Info Sheet 2026):
> «Create a product or service that uses technology to foster genuine human connection
> and/or counter the growing loneliness in our digital society.»

Format: **3 min pitch + 3 slides + 2 min Q&A**, EN or Standard German, slot 3:30–4:30 pm.
The finalists pitch the same version again, so **what you pitch first is final.**

## 1. Our idea (from the whiteboards)
An add-on to maps apps. When you're already going somewhere (lunch at a restaurant, a train
ride), the app offers to seat you **at the same table** as someone with **similar interests**
but a **different age or background**. It gives you a conversation starter. Because people
meet **repeatedly**, weak ties can grow into strong ones. Restaurants and SBB are partners.
- Vision: less loneliness (Einsamkeit), everyone feels connected
- Mission: strengthen and create weak ties, as easy as possible
- Values: human-oriented; the digital platform is the means, not the end
- Revenue: restaurants pay; low-cost software

## 2. Honest assessment
**Strong points**
- Fits the brief exactly: the tech gets you off the screen and into a real encounter.
- **"No extra effort, minimal screen time"** is the key insight. Most competitors need a new
  app plus planning. We hook into a moment that already happens (lunch, commute).
- **Mixed ages and backgrounds / reducing polarisation** adds depth and gives social sustainability.
- **Repetition** is backed by research, which is rare in a pitch. Use it.

**Gaps the jury will spot (fix them now)**
| Gap | Fix |
|---|---|
| **Competitor: Timeleft** (dinners with 6 strangers, runs in Zurich, Geneva and other cities) | Timeleft = planned evening event, one-off, like-minded people. **Ours = everyday moments, zero planning, same people again, deliberately mixed.** Also: Meetup, Bumble BFF, communal tables, Plauderbänkli ("chat benches"). |
| **"Google Maps add-on"**: Google isn't our partner | Say: *"First as a lightweight web app linked from restaurant and SBB pages, integrated via the Maps API. Long-term vision: a feature inside maps apps."* |
| **Safety meeting strangers** (the #1 Q&A question) | Public places only, verified profiles, opt-in per visit, rating/blocking, "group of 3+" option. |
| **Privacy (location data, AI profiling)** | Location only while opted in, no tracking history, interests entered by the user, Swiss hosting, compliant with the revised Swiss Data Protection Act (revDSG). |
| **"AI rates metrics"** feels creepy | Change to a **1-tap check after the meal: "Would you like to meet again?"** It measures impact and triggers repetition. |
| **Sustainability** is only a heading on the board | See section 3. |
| **Revenue: just "restaurants pay"** | Restaurants pay **per matched guest in off-peak hours** (they only pay for results). Later: SBB, Gemeinden, health insurers. Users: free. |

## 3. Sustainability angle (a key point on the info sheet)
- **Social (primary):** loneliness is a public-health issue. The WHO calls social connection
  a global health priority, and the US Surgeon General compared the health risk of chronic
  loneliness to smoking up to 15 cigarettes a day. In the Swiss Health Survey, a large share of the
  population reports feeling lonely at least sometimes, and **young people are the most affected**.
  (Find the exact current Swiss Federal Statistical Office (BFS) figure if you can; otherwise say "around 4 in 10".)
- **Bridging groups:** deliberately mixing ages and backgrounds reduces polarisation.
- **Environmental (secondary, keep it honest):** it uses **existing** infrastructure and builds nothing new.
  Restaurants fill **empty seats in off-peak hours**, which makes better use of existing space and means
  less food wasted from unsold daily menus. Meeting locally, on foot or by train, not by car.

## 4. Three slides
**Slide 1 – Problem & Idea**
- Title: *"[Name]: Your next lunch, with someone new."*
- One number on loneliness and one photo/persona (a young person alone with a phone at lunch)
- One line: *"A maps add-on that seats you with someone who shares your interests, but not your age or background."*

**Slide 2 – How it works & why it's different**
- 3-step flow with mock-up screens: **1. Opt in** when you pick a restaurant/train → **2. Match** on
  shared interests with a different background → **3. Same table + conversation starter** → (repeat)
- A small table comparing us with Timeleft/Meetup/dating apps: *zero planning · everyday moment · repetition · diversity*
- Target group + benefit: young people who feel lonely get low-pressure, repeated contact.
  Restaurants fill off-peak seats.

**Slide 3 – Impact, business & next steps**
- Sustainability: social (loneliness, polarisation) + environmental (existing spaces, off-peak use)
- Business: free for users, restaurants pay per matched guest, costs = software only
- Challenges + answers: safety, privacy, critical mass (start in **one** district)
- Next steps: **pilot with 5 restaurants in Zurich (Kreis 4/5) + 1 SBB line**, 3 months,
  measure "would meet again" rate

## 5. 3-minute script (about 400 words)
- **0:00–0:25 Hook:** "Lena, 22, moved to Zurich for her studies. She's surrounded by people
  every day on the tram and in the canteen, and still eats lunch alone, scrolling. She's not the
  exception: young people are the loneliest age group in Switzerland."
- **0:25–1:15 Idea & how it works:** the 3 steps. Emphasise *no new app habit, no planning,
  minimal screen time*: "The technology disappears the moment the conversation starts."
- **1:15–1:45 Why it's different:** Timeleft and Meetup are events you plan. We use moments you
  already have, with the same people again, and deliberately mixed. "Research says it takes about
  50 hours together to turn an acquaintance into a friend. Repetition is the feature."
- **1:45–2:15 Sustainability:** social and environmental, as in section 3.
- **2:15–2:45 Implementation & money:** free for users, restaurants pay per guest, low-cost
  software. Challenges: safety and privacy, with our answers in one sentence each.
- **2:45–3:00 Next steps + close:** Zurich pilot, then repeat the name and vision:
  "Less Einsamkeit. Everyone feels connected."

## 6. Q&A prep (2 min, so answer each in about 20 s)
- *Is it safe?* Public places, verification, opt-in each time, blocking, group option.
- *What if I don't want to talk?* It's opt-in per visit, and there's a "just lunch" mode.
- *Why would restaurants pay?* Empty tables at 2 pm cost them money. They pay only per matched guest.
- *Why would Google/SBB work with you?* We start standalone via the API. SBB has an interest in
  customer experience and could offer a "chat compartment".
- *Why not Timeleft?* Different moment, different people, repetition.
- *Isn't an app the opposite of connection?* That's why screen time is minimal. The app is the means, not the end (your values!).
- *How do you measure success?* The "would meet again" rate, repeat meetings, and a short loneliness self-check.

## 7. Name ideas
- **Stammtisch** / **Digital Stammtisch**: the Swiss tradition of a regulars' table fits
  "same table + repetition" perfectly. The jury will get it instantly.
- **Tischgspröch**, **Plaudertisch**, **SeatShare**, **Tablemate**
